The Problem

What's broken in Pakistani pharmacy software today

Most pharmacies still run on spreadsheets, paper notebooks and software that was never designed for how they actually sell.

✕

No pre-loaded medicine data

Pharmacists type every single medicine manually before the system is even usable.

✕

Strip vs loose tracked on paper

Selling by strip or by tablet is recorded by hand, causing constant stock errors.

✕

No real profit visibility

Nobody knows the actual cost of goods sold, so margins are guessed rather than measured.

✕

Credit sales in paper notebooks

Udhaar lives in paper khata books — unverifiable, unlimited and easily lost.

The Solution

MedZone fixes all of it

Every pain point above is answered by a first-class feature — not a workaround.

✓

18,706 medicines on day one

A full Pakistani medicine catalogue ships with the system — search and add, never retype.

✓

Native strip & loose units

Stock is modelled in strips and tablets at once, so every sale stays perfectly reconciled.

✓

COGS from real batch costs

Profit is computed from the landed cost of the exact batch sold — actual numbers, not estimates.

✓

Digital Udhaar ledger

Credit customers get a digital khata with enforced limits, payment history and an audit trail.
